# My Favorite Series & Movies

## 🧟 Zombies: World War Z and The Walking Dead

### World War Z {#wwz}

**World War Z** is a 2013 zombie movie starring *Brad Pitt*. It is based on the novel by Max Brooks.

> The zombies in this movie are fast, and they swarm like a wave.

- Directed by Marc Forster
- Main character: Gerry Lane
- Famous for the huge zombie swarm scenes

### The Walking Dead

**The Walking Dead** is a post-apocalyptic TV series based on the comic by Robert Kirkman. It ran for 11 seasons.

1. Rick Grimes wakes up from a coma
2. He finds the world full of walkers
3. He builds a group to survive

Zombies are called walkers. The term `walker` is used in the series instead of "zombie".
